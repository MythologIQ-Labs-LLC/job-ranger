# Code of Conduct

Sojourner welcomes constructive participation from people with different backgrounds, skill levels, and professional experiences.

## Expected Behavior

Participants should:

- communicate respectfully and focus criticism on ideas, code, documentation, or behavior;
- assume good intent without requiring others to tolerate repeated harmful behavior;
- make technical disagreement specific and evidence-based;
- respect privacy and avoid publishing personal information without permission;
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, or targeted hostility;
- make space for contributors who are newer to software development or open source.

## Project-Specific Standard

Sojourner is explicitly intended to become usable by ordinary job seekers, including people who are not developers. Dismissing accessibility, clarity, or nontechnical users as unimportant is inconsistent with the project's product direction.

## Enforcement

The repository maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, restrict participation, or block contributors when behavior materially undermines a safe and productive project environment.

Serious conduct concerns should be raised privately with the maintainer when possible. Do not publish sensitive personal information in a public issue.

## Scope

This code applies to project spaces controlled by Sojourner, including issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project communication channels.
