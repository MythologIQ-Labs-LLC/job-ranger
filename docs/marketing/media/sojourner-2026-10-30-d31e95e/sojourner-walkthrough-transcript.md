# Sojourner: Employment Toolkit: walkthrough transcript

**Video:** `sojourner-walkthrough.webm` / `sojourner-walkthrough.mp4` (81.88 seconds, 1600×900, Canopy theme).

**Audio:** none. The video has no audio track and no narration. This transcript describes what is shown; quoted text appears on screen.

**Data:** every person, employer, job listing, email address and record shown is fictional demo data. The listing is seeded for the demo and served locally during recording; no live job feed is used.

**Recording overlays:** three elements are added by the recording script:
- the opening title card;
- the short captions in the lower-left corner;
- the cursor ring.

Everything else is the running Sojourner web app.

## Text alternative (short)

An 82-second silent walkthrough of the Sojourner web app using fictional data:
1. The user opens a target career direction.
2. They review one job listing. Its assessment keeps eligibility, evidence, career alignment, preferences and unknowns separate.
3. They check their confirmed Career Evidence.
4. They prepare a resume built only from confirmed facts. It passes the Truth Gate and is exported.
5. In their own tracker, they record the job's status as Applied. The exported resume is linked to that application. Nothing is submitted to an employer.
